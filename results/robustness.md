## B. Why the section 1 reading is withdrawn

- Present in Dolma v1.7 (dose_dolma >= 1): **96.7%** of sentences in the NatInst files (n = 6,735) vs **2.0%** of the rest (n = 2,599).
- corr(log1p dose_dolma, log1p dose_natinst) = 0.79; VIF of dose_natinst in the section A model = 2.8.
- So `dose_dolma` is predominantly the injected route measured in the training corpus,
  not 'general v1.7 exposure'. The coefficient on `dose_natinst` holding `dose_dolma` fixed
  is a contrast between two strongly correlated counts of the same copies, and cannot show
  that the route leaves likelihood unmoved. The planned tests in section C do not depend on it.

### D4. How far could exposure have moved the CrowS score?

Under the planned scorers, 102 of 1,508 CrowS pairs are exact ties: nothing is
scored after the swapped word, and the scored tokens before it share a left context, so
both members get identical log-probabilities in any causal LM. No exposure effect can
break such a tie, so ties are held fixed below. The planned score counts ties as
non-stereotypical; excluding them moves OLMo v1.7 from 62.86 to 67.43. The full-sentence scorer has 0 ties.

The H3 DiD does not exclude the planned +0.2, so state the null as a bound: remove an
exposure effect at either end of the DiD's 95% CI (widest of the bias_type / pair / writer
SEs) from OLMo v1.7's untied pair differences and recompute its CrowS score.
